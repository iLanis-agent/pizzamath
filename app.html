🍕 PizzaMath

Pizza dough math that holds up. Balls, percentages, ferment - numbers you can plan a pizza night on.

Dough ball weight

Ball weight follows pie area, and style sets the grams per square inch. Neapolitan stretches thin; pan goes deep.

Dough recipe from total weight

Baker's percentages backwards: give it the dough you need, get flour, water, salt and yeast.

Sauce & cheese

Toppings scale with area, not diameter. A 16in pie needs nearly double a 12in.

Oven honesty

What each style actually wants from your oven, stated plainly.
